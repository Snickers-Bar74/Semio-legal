# Delete Semio data

Semio has no user account to delete. Progress, streaks, settings, notes, review
schedules and code drafts are stored on your phone.

## Remove local data

Use Android's **Settings → Apps → Semio → Storage → Clear storage**, or uninstall
the app. Device labels vary. This removes the live local app data and cannot be
undone through Semio. Save any code or notes you want to keep first.

Android may keep a system backup or device-transfer copy and may restore it
after reinstallation. Manage those copies through your Android/Google backup
settings; Semio does not control them.

## Usage counts and crash reports

Turn off **Settings → Share usage counts** to stop future optional counts. This
does not recall counts already delivered or turn off the separate crash reports.
The privacy policy describes what those reports contain and their retention.

For a privacy or deletion request, email
[jonathan@nullmening.org](mailto:jonathan@nullmening.org?subject=Semio%20data%20deletion)
with the subject **Semio data deletion**. Say which data concerns you and the
approximate date and app version if known. Do not send private code, tokens or
passwords. Usage counts have no persistent account identifier, so we may be
unable to identify a particular person's counts. We will assess what can be
located without collecting unnecessary identifying information.
