# Semio support

Email [jonathan@nullmening.org](mailto:jonathan@nullmening.org?subject=Semio%20support)
with the subject **Semio support**. Include your app version, Android version,
phone model, lesson or exercise, and what happened. For a lesson error, describe
the expected answer and what Semio accepted or rejected. Do not send private
code or personal information in screenshots.

Semio's lesson and playground code runs on your device. Its simulated examples
do not necessarily behave like a production browser or service. Progress and
drafts are local; clearing app storage can erase them.
