# Semio terms of use

**Pre-launch draft, prepared 28 October 2026. These proposed terms have no
effective date and must be reviewed before launch.**

[jonathan@nullmening.org](mailto:jonathan@nullmening.org). Semio provides
JavaScript lessons, practice, exercises and an on-device playground.

Semio is a learning aid. It does not promise a qualification, employment or a
particular learning outcome. Examples and simulated APIs are for practice;
review and test code independently before using it in a real application.

the app does not grant permission to republish its course or branding. Nothing
here restricts rights granted by an applicable open-source or third-party license.

Do not use the app to infringe others' rights or attempt to bypass its execution
limits or security. Keep your own copies of code and notes you need: clearing
storage can remove progress and drafts.

## Availability and your rights

Features and course content may change. Device behavior and interrupted storage
can affect availability. No term excludes mandatory consumer rights or privacy
rights. Norwegian law is proposed, subject to mandatory protections where you live.

The intended audience and store age classification must be settled before these
terms take effect. This draft creates no new age restriction or paid plan.

## Contact and changes

Use the contact above for support, course corrections or privacy questions.
Approved launch terms will carry an effective date and material changes will
be communicated where required.
